# Repository licensing direction

Status: owner-approved direction; scoped license files pending
Updated: 2026-07-29

The project intends to license the independently authored modern application,
domain and data-pipeline packages, supporting scripts, documentation, and
synthetic fixtures under the MIT License.

That license must not be presented as covering:

- the preserved `legacy/` application;
- official Dungeons of Dredmor XML, text, images, sounds, or other assets;
- generated artifacts derived from official inputs;
- bundled historical mods or their assets; or
- inherited historical artwork and source files whose provenance or permission
  is unresolved.

Before adding the root license and accompanying scope notice, confirm the
copyright-holder wording. Until those files are added, this direction records
the selected license but does not broaden the rights granted by the current
repository.

The data and publication boundary remains separately governed by
[`data-and-assets-policy.md`](data-and-assets-policy.md).
