# Repeated Craft and Encrust declaration parity evidence

Date: 2026-08-30
Status: implemented and locally verified
Dataset: `1.1.5 public_beta`, Steam build `22934623`, all three official expansions

## Finding

The preserved application treats Craft and Encrust XML records as ordered
declarations. Their displayed names are not unique identities. The official
sources contain:

- 435 Craft declarations under 374 displayed names; and
- 58 Encrust declarations under 57 displayed names.

The generic modern source-precedence resolver had reduced those sets to 374
recipes and 57 encrustments. That removed 61 recipe alternatives and one
encrustment alternative from catalogues, item relationships, search, static
routes, and planners. It was not merely a count mismatch: only one of eight
`Aqua Vitae Recipe` ingredient alternatives remained, and only the Feet form
of `Rocket Thrusters` remained instead of the separate Hands and Feet
declarations.

## Implemented contract

ADR 0007 makes differing same-name Craft and Encrust declarations independent
active entities. A normalized semantic signature excludes provenance, routes,
diagnostics, and other resolution metadata. Fact-identical declarations still
coalesce by ordinary source precedence; differing declarations receive a
deterministic semantic identity before precedence resolution.

The declaration formerly selected by the generic resolver retains the plain
entity identity and clean route. This preserves `/recipes/aqua-vitae-recipe/`
for the Night Cap declaration and `/encrustments/rocket-thrusters/` for the Feet
declaration. Additional declarations receive deterministic collision-safe
routes without changing their source-visible names.

Each declaration then follows the ordinary relationship, route, search, and
planner contracts independently. Search text includes recipe ingredients and
outputs plus Encrust ingredients, applicability, and powers. Empty recipe
descriptions use an ingredient-to-output summary, and planner choices add
ingredient context only when their existing yield/name labels would otherwise
be identical.

## Canonical result

The deterministic ignored official artifact now contains:

| Surface | Complete declaration count | Distribution                                                                    |
| ------- | -------------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Craft   |                        435 | Smithing 189, Tinkerer 99, Alchemy 57, Grinder 28, Ingot 21, Still 21, Lathe 20 |
| Encrust |                         58 | Smithing 20, Tinkerer 16, Alchemy 12, Lathe 7, Ingot 3                          |
| Search  |                      2,892 | one document for every active normalized entity                                 |

The 62 additional active identities emit 62 informational
`independent_named_declaration` records. The official gate remains at zero
errors and the same four deliberate relationship warnings; the correction does
not suppress or reclassify those warnings.

Representative coverage:

- the Still catalogue exposes all eight `Aqua Vitae Recipe` declarations with
  Slivovitz, Pear Brandy, Applejack, Brandy, Dwarven Gut Rot, Absinthe,
  Bourbon, and Night Cap inputs;
- the Aqua Vitae dependency planner exposes nine exact output choices because
  the Night Cap declaration itself has two source-level output tiers;
- `/items/slivovitz/` links to the Slivovitz-based Aqua Vitae declaration under
  **Used to craft**;
- recipe search for `slivovitz` finds both the Slivovitz recipe and the
  Slivovitz-based Aqua Vitae recipe; and
- the Tinkerer catalogue exposes both `Rocket Thrusters` cards: Hands with
  `+3 Melee Power`, and Feet with `+5 Dodge Chance`.
